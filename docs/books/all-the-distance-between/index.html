All the Distance Between

The Meridian Sequence, Book 2

by Eleanor Hart

Years after the first signal from 61 Virginis, Lena Kasra faces the daunting delay of a conversation measured in decades rather than minutes. Joined by a xenolinguist and new crew members, she discovers that communication with another intelligence is less like translating words and more like negotiating understanding. Book two of the Meridian Sequence explores waiting, sacrifice, grief and the possibility that some distances cannot be eliminated.
