The Empty Cab

Sequel to The Fare

by Yusuf Hasan

After twenty-three years driving a London taxi, Yusuf Hasan retires in the shadow of an irregular heart rhythm he has concealed behind a confident farewell. Without passengers, routine or the role that made him feel needed, he must confront changes in marriage, family and identity. Visits to old colleagues and a long-estranged brother in Canada turn retirement into a difficult, moving journey from listening to other people’s truths towards telling his own.
