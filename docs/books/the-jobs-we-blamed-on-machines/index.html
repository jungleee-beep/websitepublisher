The Jobs We Blamed on Machines

by Sage Norden, PhD

Sage Norden challenges the corporate claim that layoffs are the inevitable result of artificial intelligence. The book investigates how automation can become a convenient explanation for cost-cutting strategies and management choices already in motion. It asks readers to separate genuine technological change from the financial incentives and decisions that determine whose jobs disappear.
