The First Year

by Adam Yusuf

Following the shahada, new Muslims face a year of practical, emotional and spiritual adjustments that introductory guides seldom address. Adam Yusuf discusses learning to pray, navigating conflicting advice, explaining conversion to family, separating religion from culture, encountering the mosque community and experiencing a first Ramadan. The book reassures readers that uncertainty, slow progress and honest questions can all be part of building a lasting Muslim life.
