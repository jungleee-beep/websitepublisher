The Patient Upstairs

Book 5 in Dr Claire Shaw series

by Anna Wray

She thought everything said in her consulting room was confidential.

Then Dr Claire Shaw agrees to treat the mysterious man living upstairs.

Lewis Garnett is a reclusive former sound engineer who hears things other people miss.

Soon, he begins revealing secrets about Claire's patients that he should never have known.

As a troubled marriage unravels, Claire realises someone has been listening.

A chilling psychological thriller about surveillance, betrayal and the terrifying price of misplaced trust.
