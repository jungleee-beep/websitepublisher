The Ordinary Infinite

Book 3 in The Meridian Sequence

by Eleanor Hart

In the closing volume of the Meridian Sequence, scientist Lena Kasra and the crew of Meridian II finally approach an encounter with the surviving consciousness of an ancient civilisation. The long journey brings unresolved questions of sacrifice, grief and belonging to the forefront. This reflective science-fiction finale asks whether the greatest barrier in first contact is distance in space or the difficulty of understanding another mind.
