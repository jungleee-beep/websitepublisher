The Last Appointment

Book 4 in Dr Claire Shaw series

by Anna Wray

She told her psychologist they would never meet again.

Three days later, Sophie Marr vanishes with her six-year-old niece, leaving an abandoned car and a troubling note on the Yorkshire coast.

Police fear the worst. But Dr Claire Shaw remembers the strange stories Sophie told during therapy.

Was she planning to end her life, or desperately trying to protect another?

A gripping psychological thriller about hidden dangers, impossible choices and the secrets people struggle to tell.
