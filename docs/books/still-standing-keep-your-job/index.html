Still Standing: Keep Your Job

by James Whitfield

A practical guide for experienced professionals who fear that artificial intelligence may make their skills obsolete. James Whitfield shows how to assess workplace changes, strengthen professional value, use AI without abandoning judgement and build networks before a crisis arises. Actionable steps help readers choose when to adapt, negotiate or move on rather than allowing anxiety to dictate their careers.
