The Roads Not Taken

by Julian Vale

What might have been? When is it too late to begin again? How much of life do we lose waiting for it to start?

The Roads Not Taken is a collection of standalone novels by Julian Vale about ordinary people at the moment everything shifts. A woman finds seven doors, each opening onto a life she could have lived. A clockmaker can mend every clock but his own, stopped the day his son left. A bride-to-be boards a night train with a letter her mother never sent. An old couple tend their garden while the young man across the street learns what they're planting in him.

Each book is complete on its own, so start anywhere. Together they explore memory, regret, second chances and the small choices that shape a whole life.

For readers of The Midnight Library, A Man Called Ove and The Unlikely Pilgrimage of Harold Fry.
