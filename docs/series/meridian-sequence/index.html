The Meridian Sequence

by Eleanor Hart

A three-book science-fiction series by Eleanor Hart following astronomer Dr Lena Kasra and the crew of the survey ship Meridian as they trace an impossible signal and approach first contact. Best read in order.

Weight of Light

The Meridian Sequence, Book 1

A literary exploration of grief, hope and the paradoxes of human connection. Its central tension lies in how even moments of beauty can carry emotional weight.
