Dr. Yusuf Al-Qalb

Non-fiction · 15 books

Dr. Yusuf Al-Qalb writes Islamic non-fiction. His 15 books include the ten-booklet Returning to Allah series, covering du'a, sabr, gratitude and more, as well as Before You Go, a guide to preparing for death, The Man They Could Not Stop, on the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), and The Daily Heart, a year of daily reflections and journaling.

Contact the Author: dryusufqalb@gmail.com
