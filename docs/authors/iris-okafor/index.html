Iris Okafor

Children's picture books

Bedtime stories with big hearts and bigger animals.

Iris Okafor writes and illustrates picture books for ages 3 to 7. Her stories are about kindness, curiosity and the occasional grumpy hippo.

She is a primary school teacher in Manchester and tests every book on her own class first.
