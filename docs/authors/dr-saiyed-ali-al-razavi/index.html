Dr. Saiyed Ali Al-Razavi writes non-fiction on Shia Islam. His 10 books include histories such as The Field of Karbala and Tears of the Ummah, guides such as The Shia Muslim Handbook and Raising Shia Children in the West, and Whispers to the Divine, a guide to popular Shia supplications.
