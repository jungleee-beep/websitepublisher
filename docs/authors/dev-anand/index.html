Dev Anand

Science fiction

Quiet, human stories about the people who keep starships running.

Dev Anand is a former marine engineer turned science fiction writer. His novels follow ordinary crews doing difficult work far from home.

He writes on early mornings, before the day job, and posts a free short story to his newsletter every month.
