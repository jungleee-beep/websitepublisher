Maren Holt

Cozy mystery

Small-town secrets, strong tea, and one very nosy librarian.

Maren Holt writes cozy mysteries set in a fictional fishing village on the Cornish coast. Before writing full time she spent fifteen years as a school librarian, which explains a lot about her detectives.

She lives with two cats and an unreasonable number of teapots.
