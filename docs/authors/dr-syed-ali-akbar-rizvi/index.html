Dr. Syed Ali Akbar Rizvi

Fiction and non-fiction · 6 books

Dr. Syed Ali Akbar Rizvi writes both non-fiction and fiction. His non-fiction includes a journey through Nahjul Balagha, a look at the prophets shared by the Bible and the Qur'an, and 365 days of reflections. His novels include The Lost and Found and its sequel, Ninety Days More.

Contact the Author: drrizviauthor@gmail.com
